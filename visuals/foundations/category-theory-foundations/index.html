Category Theory Axioms

Commutative Diagram: A directed diagram of objects and arrows commutes iff for any two paths between the same pair of objects, the composite morphisms are identical.

Monomorphism (Monic): A morphism $f: X \to Y$ is monic iff it satisfies left-cancellation: $f \circ g_1 = f \circ g_2 \implies g_1 = g_2$ for all test probes $g_1, g_2: W \to X$. In $\mathbf{Set}$, monic $\iff$ injective.

Epimorphism (Epic): A morphism $f: X \to Y$ is epic iff it satisfies right-cancellation: $h_1 \circ f = h_2 \circ f \implies h_1 = h_2$ for all test probes $h_1, h_2: Y \to Z$. In $\mathbf{Set}$, epic $\iff$ surjective.

Universal Quotient Factorization: Every morphism $f: X \to Y$ factors through the canonical projection $\pi: X \to X/\ker(f)$, the induced isomorphism $\bar{f}: X/\ker(f) \to \operatorname{im}(f)$, and inclusion $\iota: \operatorname{im}(f) \hookrightarrow Y$.

Cartesian Adjunction: In Cartesian closed categories, taking the product is left adjoint to function exponentiation: $\operatorname{Hom}(A \times B, C) \cong \operatorname{Hom}(A, C^B)$.
