Category Theory Axioms

Commutative Diagram: A directed diagram of objects and arrows commutes iff for any two paths between the same pair of objects, the composite morphisms are identical.

Monomorphism (Monic): A morphism f: X → Y is monic iff it satisfies left-cancellation: f ∁ g1 = f ∁ g2 ⇒ g1 = g2 for all test probes g1, g2: W → X. In Set, monic ⇔ injective.

Epimorphism (Epic): A morphism f: X → Y is epic iff it satisfies right-cancellation: h1 ∁ f = h2 ∁ f ⇒ h1 = h2 for all test probes h1, h2: Y → Z. In Set, epic ⇔ surjective.

Universal Quotient Factorization: Every morphism f: X → Y factors through the canonical projection π: X → X/ker(f), the induced isomorphism f: X/ker(f) → im(f), and inclusion ι: im(f) → Y.

Cartesian Adjunction: In Cartesian closed categories, taking the product is left adjoint to function exponentiation: Hom(A × B, C) ≅ Hom(A, CB).
