Theory & Group Axioms

Symmetric Group (Sn, ∁): The set of all bijections σ: {1, …, n} → {1, …, n} under function composition forms a non-abelian group of size |Sn| = n!.

Cycle Order: The order of σ is the smallest integer k ≥ 1 such that σk = id. In disjoint cycle notation, ord(σ) = lcm(|c1|, |c2|, …, |cm|).

Transpositions & Parity: Every permutation decomposes into a product of 2-cycles (i j). While the factorization is not unique, its parity mod 2 is invariant: sgn(σ) = (-1)transpositions = (-1)inversions.

Alternating Group An: The subgroup of even permutations has index 2 with |An| = n! / 2.
