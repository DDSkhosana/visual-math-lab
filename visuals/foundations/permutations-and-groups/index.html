Theory & Group Axioms

Symmetric Group $(S_n, \circ)$: The set of all bijections $\sigma: \{1, \dots, n\} \to \{1, \dots, n\}$ under function composition forms a non-abelian group of size $|S_n| = n!$.

Cycle Order: The order of $\sigma$ is the smallest integer $k \ge 1$ such that $\sigma^k = \operatorname{id}$. In disjoint cycle notation, $\operatorname{ord}(\sigma) = \operatorname{lcm}(|c_1|, |c_2|, \dots, |c_m|)$.

Transpositions & Parity: Every permutation decomposes into a product of 2-cycles $(i \, j)$. While the factorization is not unique, its parity mod 2 is invariant: $\operatorname{sgn}(\sigma) = (-1)^{\text{transpositions}} = (-1)^{\text{inversions}}$.

Alternating Group $A_n$: The subgroup of even permutations has index 2 with $|A_n| = \frac{n!}{2}$.
