Axioms & The Partition Theorem

Kuratowski Ordered Pair: $(a, b) := \{\{a\}, \{a, b\}\}$. A binary relation $R$ on $A$ is any subset $R \subseteq A \times A$.

1. Reflexivity: $\forall a \in A, \, (a, a) \in R$ (all diagonal matrix entries $M_{ii} = 1$).

2. Symmetry: $\forall a, b, \, (a, b) \in R \implies (b, a) \in R$ ($M = M^T$).

3. Antisymmetry: $\forall a \neq b, \, \neg((a, b) \in R \land (b, a) \in R)$ (no bidirectional distinct edges).

4. Transitivity: $\forall a, b, c, \, [(a, b) \in R \land (b, c) \in R] \implies (a, c) \in R$ ($M^2 \le M$).

Partition Theorem: An equivalence relation $\sim$ partitions $A$ into equivalence classes $[a] := \{x \in A : x \sim a\}$. Distinct classes are strictly disjoint, and their union recovers $A$.
