Axioms & The Partition Theorem

Kuratowski Ordered Pair: (a, b) := {{a}, {a, b}}. A binary relation R on A is any subset R ⊆ A × A.

1. Reflexivity: ∀a ∈ A, (a, a) ∈ R (all diagonal matrix entries Mii = 1).

2. Symmetry: ∀a, b, (a, b) ∈ R ⇒ (b, a) ∈ R (M = MT).

3. Antisymmetry: ∀a ≠ b, ¬((a, b) ∈ R ∧ (b, a) ∈ R) (no bidirectional distinct edges).

4. Transitivity: ∀a, b, c, [(a, b) ∈ R ∧ (b, c) ∈ R] ⇒ (a, c) ∈ R (M² ≤ M).

Partition Theorem: An equivalence relation ~ partitions A into equivalence classes [a] := {x ∈ A : x ~ a}. Distinct classes are strictly disjoint, and their union recovers A.
