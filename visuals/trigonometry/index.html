Suite 02

Law of Sines & Circumcircle Invariant

Drag vertices A, B, and C around the circumcircle to verify why each chord divided by the sine of its opposite angle equals the circumdiameter 2R via Thales' antipodal right-triangle bridge.

a / sin A = b / sin B = c / sin C = 2R
Suite 03

Law of Cosines & Generalized Pythagorean Projection

Decompose acute, right, and obtuse triangles using idempotent orthogonal projection P² = P and extract the −2ab cos C bilinear interaction term via the 4-corner difference quotient.

c² = a² + b² − 2ab cos C
Suite 04

Angle Sum, Difference & Rotation Matrix Composition

Compose 2×2 rotation operators R(α) ˆ R(β) = R(α + β) and inspect the stacked right-triangle geometric proof for sin(α ± β) and cos(α ± β).

R(α + β) = R(α) ˆ R(β)
