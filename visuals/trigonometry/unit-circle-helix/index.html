1. Quotient Angle & Idempotent Projections

Each angle θ ∈ ℝ / 2πℤ maps bijectively to a point P(cos θ, sin θ) on S¹. Applying orthogonal idempotent projections P_x² = P_x and P_y² = P_y decomposes OP into cos θ and sin θ:
cos²θ + sin²θ = 1.0000

2. Exterior Tangent & Reciprocal Secant/Cosecant

The tangent line to the unit circle at P(cos θ, sin θ) has equation x cos θ + y sin θ = 1. Its X-intercept is (sec θ, 0) and its Y-intercept is (0, csc θ):
1 + tan²θ = sec²θ

3. 3D Cylindrical Helix Wave Projections

Lifting P(cos θ, sin θ) along the axial parameter z = kθ generates the 3D helix r(θ) = (R cos θ, R sin θ, kθ). Its orthogonal XZ floor projection is the cosine wave, and its YZ wall projection is the sine wave.
