1. Group Homomorphism R : (ℝ/2πℤ, +) → SO(2)

Rotating the plane by β and then by α is a composition of linear maps R(α) ∘ R(β) = R(α + β):
[cos(α+β) −sin(α+β)] = [cos α −sin α][cos β −sin β]

2. Stacked Right-Triangle Geometric Proof

Hypotenuse OP = 1 splits along ray α into OQ = cos β and QP = sin β. Projecting OQ and QP onto the X and Y axes yields:
cos(α + β) = OM − SQ = cos α cos β − sin α sin β
sin(α + β) = MQ + PS = sin α cos β + cos α sin β
