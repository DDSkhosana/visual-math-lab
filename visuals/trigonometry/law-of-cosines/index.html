1. Idempotent Orthogonal Split (P_∥ + P_⊥ = I)

Projecting vertex B onto line CA via P_∥ yields foot D with signed coordinate p = a cos C and orthogonal altitude h = a sin C:
c² = (a sin C)² + (b − a cos C)² = a² + b² − 2ab cos C

2. 4-Corner Mixed Partial Difference Quotient

Applying the 4-corner rectangle difference quotient [f(a,b) − f(a,0) − f(0,b) + f(0,0)] / (ab) to f(a,b) = c²(a,b) cancels the pure a² and b² terms and isolates the exact bilinear interaction coefficient:
∂²(c²)/(∂a ∂b) = −2 cos C
