1. Altitude Decomposition (Idempotent Projection)

Projecting C orthogonally onto AB yields altitude h_c. In right triangles ACH_c and BCH_c:
h_c = b sin A = a sin B ⇒ a / sin A = b / sin B

2. Thales' Diameter Bridge to 2R

Drawing diameter A′B = 2R through circumcenter O creates inscribed angle ∠A′CB = 90° (by Thales' Theorem) and ∠BA′C = ∠BAC = A (angles in the same segment). Thus in right △A′BC:
sin A = sin A′ = a / (2R) ⇒ a / sin A = 2R
