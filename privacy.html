1. No User Accounts or Personal Profiles

Every interactive visualisation, 2D/3D proof, and derivation calculator on this platform is accessible immediately without registering, creating an account, or providing personal credentials.

2. Client-Side Mathematical Execution & Hardware Isolation

All geometric calculations, matrix transformations, coordinate updates, WebGL/Three.js shaders, and angle derivations execute 100% within your device's browser using HTML5 Canvas and WebGL. No user geometry configurations, slider parameters, or coordinates are ever transmitted to or stored on our servers.

Camera Hardware Access: When entering Augmented Reality (AR) mode, the application requests access to your camera solely to render the live physical background behind 3D models. Camera access requires your explicit browser-level permission each time. The video feed is processed locally in memory in real time and is never recorded, saved, or transmitted across any network.

3. Aggregated Analytics & Google Consent Mode v2

We use Google Analytics under Google Consent Mode v2. Technical analytics are active by default as a necessary operational feature to monitor application performance, display responsiveness, and mathematical rendering accuracy across devices. If you prefer not to participate, you may explicitly opt out at any time through our cookie notice or the preferences manager above.

Opting out immediately signals Google Consent Mode (analytics_storage: 'denied'), disabling analytics cookies and telemetry. When active, analytics data is strictly aggregated and anonymized. All advertising storage (ad_storage, ad_user_data, ad_personalization) and Google Signals are permanently disabled, and IP addresses are strictly anonymized.

5. European Union & United Kingdom Compliance (GDPR & ePrivacy)

Under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the UK Data Protection Act 2018:

  • Lawful Basis for Analytics: We process aggregated technical telemetry based on legitimate interests for application reliability and mathematical verification (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR), as well as explicit consent where confirmed (Article 6(1)(a) GDPR), with an unconditional right to opt out at any time.
  • Data Subject Rights: You have the right to access, rectify, or request erasure of personal data, as well as the right to restrict or object to processing. You may withdraw consent at any time using the preferences button above or the "Cookie Settings" link in the footer.
  • Supervisory Authority: You have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority, such as the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) in the UK or the relevant Data Protection Authority (DPA) in your EU member state.

7. United States State Privacy Laws (CCPA / CPRA & State Statutes)

"Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information": Visual Mathematics Lab does not sell your personal information, nor do we share personal information with third parties for cross-context behavioral advertising, as defined by the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) and the California Privacy Rights Act (CPRA). We respect browser-level Global Privacy Control (GPC) opt-out signals.

8. COPPA & Student Privacy Protection

Visual Mathematics Lab is designed for educational use across primary, secondary, and higher education. In compliance with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA):

  • We do not collect personal names, email addresses, physical addresses, or phone numbers from children under 13.
  • Google Signals and ad personalization features are permanently disabled.
  • IP addresses are anonymized before any analytics records are processed.
  • All geometric simulations and math derivations run locally without external data exchange.

9. Security Architecture & Subresource Integrity (SRI)

To guard against supply-chain tampering and content injection:

  • All external JavaScript libraries (KaTeX, Three.js, OrbitControls) are verified using Subresource Integrity (SRI) SHA-384 cryptographic hashes.
  • HTTP headers and metadata enforce a strict Content Security Policy (CSP) and Permissions-Policy, restricting execution strictly to approved domains and disallowing unauthorized camera, microphone, or geolocation access.
  • All network communication is enforced over TLS 1.3 / HTTPS with HSTS.
