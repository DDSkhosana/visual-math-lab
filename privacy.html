Privacy Policy

Privacy with restraint.

Visual Mathematics Lab is an open educational laboratory. We practice data restraint: no user accounts, no advertising trackers, and no sale of personal data.

1. No User Accounts or Personal Profiles

Every interactive visualisation, 2D/3D proof, and derivation calculator on this platform is accessible immediately without registering, creating an account, or providing personal credentials.

2. Client-Side Mathematical Execution

All geometric calculations, matrix transformations, coordinate updates, and angle derivations run strictly within your device's browser using HTML5 Canvas and WebGL. No user geometry configurations or interaction coordinates are ever transmitted to or stored on our servers.

3. Aggregated Analytics

We use Google Analytics 4 (Measurement ID: G-E1W7GYR7Q0) to observe aggregated, non-identifying technical usage—such as page visits, theorem interaction events, and viewport dimensions. This helps us ensure compatibility across mobile, tablet, and desktop screens. We do not use analytics for behavioral profiling or advertising.

4. Third-Party Services

Visual Mathematics Lab interacts with third-party providers only for specific, user-initiated actions:

  • Project Support: Voluntary financial contributions are processed externally through Buy Me a Coffee. Visual Mathematics Lab does not collect, receive, or store payment card numbers or billing details.
  • Inquiries & Feedback: Direct inquiries submitted via our Contact page are processed through Google Forms and are used solely to reply to your message.
  • Educational Media: Any linked video demonstrations are hosted on YouTube and operate under Google's Privacy Policy.

5. Local Storage & Cookies

We do not deploy marketing or cross-site tracking cookies. Purely functional client-side storage (such as remembering your active viewport mode or measurement unit preferences) may be retained locally in your browser cache.

6. Contact

If you have any questions about this privacy policy or our data practices, please reach out through our contact channel.
